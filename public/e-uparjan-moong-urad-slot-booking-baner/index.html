✨E Uparjan Moong Urad Slot Booking Baner Key Highlights

  • Online slot booking for Wheat (Gehu) and Paddy (Dhan).
  • Sell crops at government procurement centers.
  • Farmer Code, Registered Mobile Number, or Samagra ID can be used.
  • Registered mobile number must remain active for OTP verification.

📌 Important Details of E Uparjan Moong Urad Slot Booking Baner

  • Eligible farmers should complete slot booking before visiting the procurement center.
  • Keep Farmer Code, Registered Mobile Number, or Samagra ID ready.
  • OTP verification requires an active registered mobile number.
  • Use the official MP e-Uparjan portal for slot booking.

📖 About E Uparjan Moong Urad Slot Booking Baner Poster

The Madhya Pradesh e-Uparjan portal provides a digital platform for farmers to book procurement slots for selling wheat and paddy under the government procurement process.

Online slot booking helps reduce waiting time, improves transparency, and simplifies crop procurement for registered farmers.
