📌 Important Details of Cm Beej Anudan Yojna Mp

  • Aadhaar Card is mandatory.
  • Mobile number must be linked with Aadhaar.
  • Email ID can be provided if available.
  • Farmer's agricultural land must be linked with Farmer ID.

📖 About Cm Beej Anudan Yojna Mp Poster

The poster spreads awareness about the Mukhyamantri Kisan Beej Anudan Yojana launched by the Department of Farmers Welfare and Agriculture Development, Government of Madhya Pradesh.

It informs farmers about essential documents, online registration, quality seed availability, and Farmer ID linkage before applying.
