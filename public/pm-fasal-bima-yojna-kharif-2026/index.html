📌 Important Details of Pm Fasal Bima Yojna Kharif 2026

  • Required Documents: Aadhaar Card, Bank Account, Khasra Number, Mobile Number and Crop Sowing Certificate/Self Declaration.
  • Apply before 31 July 2026 to receive crop insurance coverage.
  • PMFBY provides financial support against crop losses caused by natural calamities, pests and diseases.
  • Keep all required documents ready before submitting the application.

📖 About Pm Fasal Bima Yojna Kharif 2026 Poster

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is a flagship crop insurance scheme launched by the Government of India to provide financial security to farmers against crop losses.

For the Kharif 2026 season, eligible farmers can insure their crops by paying only 2% of the insured amount as premium before the last date of 31 July 2026.
