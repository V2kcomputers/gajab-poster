Protect Your Crops with Farm Fencing Assistance

✨Mp Taar Fensing Yojna Key Highlights

  • Up to 50% subsidy
  • Crop protection from animals
  • Running fencing assistance from 200 meters to 1000 meters
  • Assistance up to ₹1.50 lakh

📌 Important Details of Mp Taar Fensing Yojna

  • Farmers can receive financial assistance for wire fencing around agricultural fields.
  • The main objective is to protect crops from stray animals and wild animals.
  • Fencing assistance is mentioned for 200 meters to 1000 meters of running length.
  • The poster mentions financial assistance of up to ₹1.50 lakh.

📖 About Mp Taar Fensing Yojna Poster

This poster provides information about the Tar Fencing Scheme associated with the Horticulture and Food Processing Department, Madhya Pradesh.

It highlights subsidy, fencing length, maximum assistance and required documents for farmers.
