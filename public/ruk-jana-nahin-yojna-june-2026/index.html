  • Online application process available

  • Application period from 25 April 2026 to 20 June 2026

📌 Important Details of Ruk Jana Nahin Yojna June 2026

  • Applicants must have failed Class 10th or Class 12th examination
  • Apply online within the prescribed dates
  • Keep required documents ready before application
  • Eligible students can appear again and continue their education

📖 About Ruk Jana Nahin Yojna June 2026 Poster

MPSOS Ruk Jana Nahi Yojana is a special educational initiative for students who could not pass their Class 10th or 12th examinations.

The scheme allows eligible students to apply online and reappear in examinations without losing an academic year.
