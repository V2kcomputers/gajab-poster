✨MP Social Security Pension Scheme Key Highlights

  • Online application facility available
  • Pension support for elderly, disabled and widows
  • Simple documentation process
  • Direct benefit transfer to bank account

📌 Important Details of MP Social Security Pension Scheme

  • Aadhaar Card is mandatory
  • Samagra ID required
  • Active bank account with passbook
  • Registered mobile number and photo needed
