📖 About PM Kisan 23 Kisht Update Poster

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi is a central government scheme providing financial assistance to eligible farmers through direct benefit transfers.

The scheme releases financial support in installments directly to farmers' bank accounts after verification and eligibility checks.
