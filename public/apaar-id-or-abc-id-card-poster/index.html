  • Educational qualification documents may be required, if applicable.
  • APAAR ID digitally stores students' academic records under the Ministry of Education.

📖 About Apaar Id or Abc Id Card Poster Poster

APAAR ID (Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry) is a unique digital identity issued to students under the Ministry of Education.

It enables secure storage and sharing of academic records across schools, colleges, and educational institutions throughout India.
