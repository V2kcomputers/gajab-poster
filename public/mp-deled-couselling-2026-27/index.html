✨MP D.El.Ed. Couselling 2026-27 Key Highlights

  • Minimum qualification is 12th pass
  • Online application starts from 15 May 2026
  • D.El.Ed is a 2-year teacher training course
  • Training is provided for teaching classes 1 to 8

📌 Important Details of MP D.El.Ed. Couselling 2026-27

  • Keep all required documents ready before registration
  • Aadhaar card and Samagra ID are mandatory
  • Mobile number and email ID are required for online registration
  • Photo and signature must be uploaded during application

📖 About MP D.El.Ed. Couselling 2026-27 Poster

MP D.El.Ed Counselling 2026-27 is conducted for admission into Diploma in Elementary Education courses.

The course prepares students for primary teacher training and teaching careers in schools.
