📌 Important Details of MP Sambal Card Yojna

  • Required documents include Aadhaar Card, Samagra ID, Mobile Number, and B1 Document (if applicable).
  • Submit the application form and supporting documents at Gram Panchayat or Nagar Parishad.
  • E-Sambal Card is issued under the Labour Department of Madhya Pradesh.
  • Benefits are available for registered unorganized workers and their families.

📖 About MP Sambal Card Yojna Poster

The Mukhyamantri Jan Kalyan Sambal Yojana is a welfare scheme launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh for unorganized workers.

Registered beneficiaries can receive financial support for maternity, death, disability, education, and other welfare-related assistance.
