📌 Important Details of MP Ladli Behna Yojna- 36th

  • Beneficiary women must have active bank accounts linked with DBT
  • Installment amount will be credited directly into bank accounts
  • Scheme is operated by Madhya Pradesh Government
  • Beneficiaries should verify bank and Aadhaar details

📖 About MP Ladli Behna Yojna- 36th Poster

Mukhyamantri Ladli Behna Yojana is an important financial assistance scheme launched by the Madhya Pradesh Government for women beneficiaries.

The scheme provides financial support directly into beneficiary bank accounts through DBT to improve women empowerment and economic stability.
