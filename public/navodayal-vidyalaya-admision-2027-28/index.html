✨Navodayal Vidyalaya Admision 2027 28 Key Highlights

  • Admission for Class 6 Entrance Exam 2027-28
  • Students studying in Class 5 are eligible to apply
  • Last date to apply: 31 July 2026
  • Entrance examination scheduled on 28 November 2026

📌 Important Details of Navodayal Vidyalaya Admision 2027 28

  • Eligibility: Student must be studying in Class 5.
  • Date of Birth: Between 01 May 2015 and 31 July 2017.
  • Required documents should be kept ready before application.
  • If Aadhaar is not linked with a mobile number, Residence Certificate is required.
