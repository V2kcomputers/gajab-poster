📌 Important Details of UDID Card - Divyang Card Poster

  • Valid disability certificate is required
  • Medical board certified disabled persons can apply
  • Aadhaar card and mobile number are mandatory
  • Photo, address proof, signature or thumb impression required

📖 About UDID Card - Divyang Card Poster Poster

UDID Card helps disabled citizens access various government schemes and services across India.

The card is issued digitally and can be downloaded or printed online after approval.
