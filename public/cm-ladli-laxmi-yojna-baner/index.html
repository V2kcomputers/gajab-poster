✨Cm Ladli Laxmi Yojna Baner Key Highlights

  • Online application available for eligible beneficiaries.
  • Financial assistance assurance up to ₹1,43,000 for eligible girl child.
  • Benefits linked with admission in Classes 6, 9, 11 and 12.
  • Applicable for girl children who are residents of Madhya Pradesh.

📌 Important Details of Cm Ladli Laxmi Yojna Baner

  • Applicant girl must be a resident of Madhya Pradesh.
  • Financial assistance up to ₹1,43,000 under the scheme.
  • Educational incentive is provided at admission in Classes 6, 9, 11 and 12.
  • Application requires prescribed supporting documents.

📖 About Cm Ladli Laxmi Yojna Baner Poster

Ladli Laxmi Yojana is designed to improve the social and educational status of girl children in Madhya Pradesh.

The scheme encourages education by providing financial benefits at different stages of schooling.
