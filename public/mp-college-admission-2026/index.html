✨MP College Admission 2026 Key Highlights

  • Online application starts from 01-05-2026
  • Document verification from 02-05-2026 to 16-05-2026
  • Seat allotment on 20-05-2026
  • Fee payment from 20-05-2026 to 26-05-2026

📌 Important Details of MP College Admission 2026

  • Students who passed 12th class can apply for undergraduate admission
  • Supplementary passed students are also eligible for application
  • Required documents include Aadhaar card, marksheets, caste and income certificates
  • ABC / APAAR ID and active mobile number are required during application
