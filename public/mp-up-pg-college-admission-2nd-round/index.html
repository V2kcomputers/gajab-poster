✨MP UG & PG College Admission 2026 - 2nd Round Key Highlights

  • Second phase registration has started for MP College Admission 2026-27
  • UG courses available after 12th pass including BA, BSc, BCom and BA LLB
  • PG courses available after graduation including MA, MSc, MCom and B.Lib
  • Online registration process is active for Madhya Pradesh colleges

📌 Important Details of MP UG & PG College Admission 2026 - 2nd Round

  • Application dates: 21/05/2026 to 03/06/2026
  • Document verification dates: 22/05/2026 to 04/06/2026
  • Admission fee submission dates: 07/06/2026 to 13/06/2026
  • Required documents include marksheets, Aadhaar card, Samagra ID, certificates, mobile number and ABC/APAAR ID

📖 About MP UG & PG College Admission 2026 - 2nd Round Poster

MP College Admission 2026-27 registration process has started for Undergraduate and Postgraduate programs in government and private colleges across Madhya Pradesh.

Students can complete online registration using required documents and participate in counselling, verification and seat allotment process.
