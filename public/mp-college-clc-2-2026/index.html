✨Mp College Clc 2 2026 Key Highlights

  • CLC Round 2: 01 July 2026 to 15 July 2026
  • College Registration till 3:00 PM daily
  • Document Verification till 4:00 PM daily
  • Seat Allotment at 5:00 PM daily

📌 Important Details of Mp College Clc 2 2026

  • Admission fee must be deposited by 4:00 PM on the day after seat allotment.
  • Required documents include Samagra ID, Aadhaar Card, Academic Mark Sheets and ABC/APAAR ID.
  • Applicable for UG and PG College Admissions.
  • Failure to deposit the admission fee within the prescribed time may lead to automatic cancellation of admission.
