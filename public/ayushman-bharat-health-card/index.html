✨Ayushman Bharat Health Card Key Highlights

  • Free treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family every year
  • Cashless treatment facility available
  • Treatment available in government and private hospitals
  • Coverage for pre-existing diseases
  • Operations, medicines and hospitalization included
  • Ayushman Card valid across India
  • Benefits available for all family members

📌 Important Details of Ayushman Bharat Health Card

  • Eligible families can apply for Ayushman Card online
  • e-KYC and card update services available
  • Eligibility checking facility available
  • Card download and print service available
  • Carry Aadhaar Card and Mobile Number for verification
  • Applicable in empanelled hospitals across India

📖 About Ayushman Bharat Health Card Poster

Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is one of India's largest government health insurance schemes for poor and eligible families.

The scheme offers cashless healthcare services in both government and private hospitals across the country.
