✨Azim Premji Scholarship 2026 Key Highlights

  • Eligibility: Passed Class 12 from a government school
  • Admission: Regular first-year admission in any college
  • Scholarship: ₹30,000 per year
  • Application Period: 10 August 2026 to 31 August 2026

📌 Important Details of Azim Premji Scholarship 2026

  • Student must be admitted regularly in the first year of a graduation or diploma course during the 2026-27 academic session.
  • Admission should be in a recognized college or university.
  • Required documents include Class 10 and 12 marksheets, Bonafide Certificate, first-year fee receipt, mobile number, bank account details, email ID, photograph and signature.
  • The banner mentions the first phase online application for the 2026-27 session.
