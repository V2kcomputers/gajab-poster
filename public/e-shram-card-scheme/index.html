✨E Shram Card Scheme Key Highlights

  • Up to ₹2 lakh accident insurance coverage
  • Access to multiple government welfare schemes
  • Single unified card for unorganized workers
  • Easy application and update process

📌 Important Details of E Shram Card Scheme

  • Required documents include Aadhaar card
  • Active mobile number is necessary
  • Bank account details must be provided
  • Nominee details are required for benefits

📖 About E Shram Card Scheme Poster

The e-Shram Card scheme is launched by the Government of India to provide social security and welfare benefits to unorganized sector workers.

It helps workers like laborers, farmers, street vendors, and small business owners access insurance and government schemes easily.
