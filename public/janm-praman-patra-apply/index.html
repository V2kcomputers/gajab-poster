✨Janm Praman Patra Apply Key Highlights

  • School and college admission use
  • Aadhaar card application support
  • Passport application requirement
  • Useful for government scheme benefits
  • Acts as identity and age proof

📌 Important Details of Janm Praman Patra Apply

  • Birth proof document such as hospital report or discharge certificate is required
  • Parents' identity proof like Aadhaar card, PAN card, Passport, or Voter ID is needed
  • Address proof such as ration card, electricity bill, or Aadhaar card is required
  • Affidavit may be required if applying after 21 days or one year of birth
