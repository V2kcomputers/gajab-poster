  • Eligible candidates: Currently teaching teachers who are not TET qualified
  • Age Limit: 18 to 40 years
  • Application Fee: ₹1060 including portal charges
  • Application Dates: 21/08/2026 to 04/09/2026

📌 Important Details of Mpesb Ms Ps Tet 2026

  • Required: MPESB Profile (or equivalent)
  • Educational marksheet: D.El.Ed / B.Ed etc.
  • Required identity and contact details: Aadhaar Card, Samagra ID, Mobile Number and Email ID
  • Other documents: Live Photo & Signature, Bank Passbook and Caste Certificate if applicable
