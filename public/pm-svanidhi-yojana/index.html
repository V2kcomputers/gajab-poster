✨Pm Svanidhi Yojana Key Highlights

  • Collateral-free loan facility for street vendors
  • Financial assistance from ₹15,000 to ₹50,000 in phases
  • Low interest rate with easy repayment options
  • Additional benefits on digital transactions

📌 Important Details of Pm Svanidhi Yojana

  • First phase loan: ₹15,000
  • Second phase loan: ₹25,000
  • Third phase loan: ₹50,000
  • Timely repayment provides additional benefits

📖 About Pm Svanidhi Yojana Poster

PM SVANidhi Yojana is a central government initiative designed to empower street vendors and small traders by providing them easy access to working capital loans.

The scheme supports self-employment and promotes financial inclusion by encouraging digital transactions and timely loan repayment.
