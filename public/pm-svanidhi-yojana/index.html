✨Pm Svanidhi Yojana Key Highlights

  • Collateral-free loan facility
  • Financial assistance from ₹10,000 to ₹50,000
  • Low interest rate benefits
  • Extra benefits on digital payments

📌 Important Details of Pm Svanidhi Yojana

  • First Phase Loan: ₹10,000
  • Second Phase Loan: ₹20,000
  • Third Phase Loan: ₹50,000
  • Quick processing and timely repayment benefits

📖 About Pm Svanidhi Yojana Poster

PM SVANidhi Yojana is designed to empower street vendors by providing easy access to working capital loans.

The scheme supports small traders, hawkers, and self-employed individuals to grow their business with government-backed financial assistance.
