✨CTET SEP 2026 Key Highlights

  • CTET 2026 examination for teaching eligibility
  • Paper-I for Classes 1 to 5
  • Paper-II for Classes 6 to 8
  • Online application from 11 May 2026 to 10 June 2026

📌 Important Details of CTET SEP 2026

  • Eligibility includes D.El.Ed / B.Ed qualification
  • 12th pass, graduation pass, or appearing candidates can apply
  • CTET examination date is 06 September 2026
  • Required documents include marksheets, Aadhaar card, photo, and signature

📖 About CTET SEP 2026 Poster
