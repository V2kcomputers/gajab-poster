✨Mp College Admission Key Highlights

  • Online admission process for UG, PG and B.Ed courses
  • First phase application for academic session 2026-27
  • Courses include BA, BSc, BCom, MA, MSc, MCom, B.Ed, M.Ed
  • Applicable after 12th and graduation as per course eligibility

📌 Important Details of Mp College Admission

  • UG Courses: After 12th – BA, BSc, BCom, BA LLB etc.
  • PG Courses: After Graduation – MA, MSc, MCom, LLM, BLib etc.
  • NCTE Courses: B.Ed, M.Ed, BA B.Ed, BSc B.Ed
  • Application Dates: UG (01 May – 15 May 2026), PG (02 May – 15 May 2026), B.Ed/M.Ed (02 May – 14 May 2026)
