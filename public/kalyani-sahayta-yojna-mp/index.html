Financial Support for Eligible Widows of Registered Workers

✨Kalyani Sahayta Yojna Mp Key Highlights

  • Annual financial assistance of ₹12,000.
  • Payment in two installments of ₹6,000 each (June & December).
  • Offline application through Labour Department or Public Service Centre.
  • Application must be submitted within one year of the worker's death.

📌 Important Details of Kalyani Sahayta Yojna Mp

  • Applicant must be a resident of Madhya Pradesh.
  • Applicant should be the widow of the deceased worker.
  • Deceased worker must have been registered with the Labour Welfare Board and worked in an eligible industrial establishment.
  • Widow should not have remarried or received similar financial assistance from another government department.

📖 About Kalyani Sahayta Yojna Mp Poster

Kalyani Sahayata Yojana is implemented by the Madhya Pradesh Labour Welfare Board to provide financial assistance to widows of eligible deceased workers.

The scheme offers ₹12,000 annually in two installments and aims to provide social and financial security to beneficiary families.
