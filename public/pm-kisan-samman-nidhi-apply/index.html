📌 Important Details of PM Kisan Samman Nidhi Apply

  • Farmers whose land is registered before 01.02.2019 may be eligible
  • Inherited agricultural land holders may also get eligibility
  • Aadhaar, Bank Account and eKYC are mandatory
  • Only cultivable agricultural land holders are eligible
